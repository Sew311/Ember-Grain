Since 2019

A shop built around one machine and one habit.

Ember & Grain started in a 40-square-foot roasting closet behind a bicycle shop. The bikes are gone now, but the habit stayed: we still roast in small batches, cup every lot before it goes on the menu, and write the origin on the bag by hand.

No syrups you can't pronounce, no espresso machine older than the person pulling the shot. Just good beans, treated properly, served by people who taste the coffee before they hand it to you.
